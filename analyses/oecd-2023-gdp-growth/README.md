# GDP Growth Across Major Economies, Latest Quarter

## Methods

Analysis of the OECD Quarterly National Accounts (QNA) dataset via the SDMX-JSON API. Year-over-year real GDP growth rates were extracted for the latest available quarter for each country.

## Results

India (7.96%), Denmark (6.19%), and Indonesia (5.46%) led GDP growth among the top 10. China grew at 5.0%, while the United States grew at approximately 2.6%.

## Limitations

- Growth rates are year-over-year and not seasonally adjusted beyond what OECD provides.
- Quarterly data may be revised in subsequent releases.
- No sampling variance; official macrodata.

## Source

OECD Statistics (SDMX-JSON API), QNA dataset. Release `oecd-2023-77d3e9c12f88`.
