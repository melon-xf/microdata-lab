# Question and estimand

## Question

Which major economies had the fastest GDP growth in the latest available quarter?

## Estimand

Year-over-year real GDP growth rate (percent change) for the top 10 OECD-member and partner countries.

## Record unit and universe

- Record unit: country-quarter observation.
- Universe: all countries reporting GDP growth to OECD QNA.
- Exclusions: none beyond missing observations.

## Variables and definitions

- `B1GQ`: Gross domestic product (expenditure approach).
- `PC`: Percent change.
- `GY`: Growth rate, year-over-year.
- `REF_AREA`: Country code (ISO 3-letter).

## Design and uncertainty

No survey design. Official macrodata — no sampling variance.

## Release

- Release ID: `oecd-2023-77d3e9c12f88`
- Source: OECD SDMX-JSON API (QNA dataset)
