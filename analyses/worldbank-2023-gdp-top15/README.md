# GDP of the 15 Largest Economies, 2023

## Methods

Analysis of World Bank Open Data API indicator NY.GDP.MKTP.CD (GDP at current market prices in US$) for 2023. Regional aggregates were excluded; only individual sovereign countries are ranked.

## Results

The United States ($27.8T) and China ($18.3T) dominate, together accounting for roughly 40% of global GDP. Japan ($4.4T) and Germany ($4.6T) round out the top five.

## Limitations

- GDP in current US$ does not adjust for purchasing power parity (PPP) or cost of living.
- No sampling variance; values are official administrative data.
- Some small economies may have estimated rather than directly reported GDP.

## Source

World Bank Open Data API (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD). Release `worldbank-2023-b913afa94f69`.
