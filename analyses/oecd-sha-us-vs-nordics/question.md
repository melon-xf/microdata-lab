# US vs Nordic health spending (OECD SHA 2023)

**Claim:** America already spends far more on health care than the
Nordics — the "we can't afford single-payer" framing is backwards; the
obstacle is allocation, not resources.

**Estimand:** total current expenditure on health as a share of GDP
(EXP_HEALTH, PT_B1GQ, FINANCING_SCHEME=_T), USA vs DNK/FIN/NOR/SWE, 2023.

**Universe:** OECD System of Health Accounts country-year observations.

**Variables:** REF_AREA, MEASURE, UNIT_MEASURE, FINANCING_SCHEME,
TIME_PERIOD, value.

**Release:** oecd_sha-2023-127dc06a8300 (5-country revision; prior
USA-only release 60b53ef2dd00 kept immutable).

**Benchmark:** US 16.87% of GDP 2023 — matches the value validated in the
earlier oecd-sha-us-financing analysis.

**Limitations:** spending share is not a quality or outcome measure;
figures are national accounts, not a prediction of any reform's cost.
