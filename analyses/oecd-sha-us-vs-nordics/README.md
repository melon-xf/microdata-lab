# US vs Nordic health spending — OECD SHA 2023

Total health expenditure as a share of GDP, 2023: US 16.87% vs Sweden
11.24%, Finland 10.50%, Denmark 9.61%, Norway 8.93% (Nordic mean 10.07%).
The US spends 6.8pp more of GDP than the Nordic mean.

Methods: OECD System of Health Accounts, EXP_HEALTH, PT_B1GQ,
FINANCING_SCHEME=_T. US figure matches the value validated in the earlier
oecd-sha-us-financing analysis (16.87%).

Limitations: spending share is not a quality or outcome measure.

Source: OECD SHA 2023, release oecd_sha-2023-127dc06a8300 (5-country
revision; the USA-only release 60b53ef2dd00 remains immutable).
