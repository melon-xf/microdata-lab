## Citation conventions in this wiki

Claims carry bracketed source keys with page refs, e.g. [S pp. 25–26],
[C p. 108], [K doc 12], [T ch. 2]. When authors conflict, the wiki
adjudicates explicitly (see 02's conflict table) — the platform
verdict is the operational rule, with the dissent preserved.
