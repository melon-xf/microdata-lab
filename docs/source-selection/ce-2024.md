# Source selection: 2024 Consumer Expenditure Surveys

Decision date: 2026-08-02

## Concept and estimand

Select the Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Expenditure Surveys (CE) 2024 Public Use Microdata for calendar-year consumer-unit expenditure flows. The first adapter benchmark estimates the 2024 mean for college tuition, UCC `670110`, from the Interview Survey. An integrated total-expenditure result is not accepted until Interview and Diary source assignments are applied from the official integration workbook.

## Comparison with local sources

- **SCF 2022:** primary economic unit balance sheet with some spending measures; triennial and not a comprehensive expenditure diary or quarterly interview. Not equivalent.
- **SIPP 2025:** person-month income, program participation, and selected medical expenditures. It does not provide the CE expenditure taxonomy. Not equivalent.
- **SHED 2025:** adult financial well-being and reported challenges. Perceived cost pressure is not an expenditure flow. Not equivalent.
- **ACS/CPS:** annual demographic and income snapshots with limited cost measures. Not equivalent.

The local catalog has no existing comprehensive consumer-unit expenditure release.

## Record unit, universe, and period

CE uses consumer units. Interview expenditures are reported over quarterly interviews and reference months; Diary expenditures come from two consecutive one-week diaries. A 2024 Interview calendar-year estimate requires five interview-quarter files: 2024 Q1 from the 2023 archive, then 2024 Q2-Q4 and 2025 Q1 from the 2024 archive. The 2024 Diary archive supplies four quarterly Diary releases.

## Weights and design

Interview point estimates use `FINLWT21` and the BLS calendar-year population-weight interpolation from the official sample program. Replicate weights are required for publishable uncertainty and are not interchangeable with `FINLWT21`. Diary and Interview estimates must use their own survey weights and source-specific records before BLS-designated UCC results are integrated.

## Source assignment and benchmark

The official integration workbook marks each UCC as Interview (`I`) or Diary (`D`) by year. For 2024, college tuition UCC `670110` is Interview-sourced. BLS Table 2500 reports a mean of `$984.77` and standard error `$79.02`. The adapter must reproduce this result within a documented tolerance before promotion. It must not benchmark a partial Interview-only reconstruction against the integrated `$78,535.21` total.

## Missingness, revisions, and limitations

Preserve BLS missing and not-applicable codes, reference year, interview timing, allocation flags, and UCC identity. Calendar-year overlap means adjacent archive releases are part of one estimand. PUMD values may differ from published estimates because of revisions, suppression, source integration, and publication processing; any tolerance must be measured from the exact official program and release, not guessed.

## Provenance decision

Use only BLS-hosted archives and documentation through the repository's host-scoped identifying User-Agent client. Never route the personal BLS identifier through Firecrawl or a global HTTP header.
