# SCF 2022 — Source Selection Note

## Source
Survey of Consumer Finances (SCF), Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System.
Summary extract public-use file: `https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/scfindex.htm`

## Access
Public download. Federal Reserve terms permit redistribution of public-use files.

## Record unit
Family (primary economic unit).

## Universe
U.S. families (non-institutionalized civilian households plus high-wealth oversample).

## Weight
`WGT` — summary-extract analysis weight. Population weight is divided across five implicates; multiply by five for population totals.

## Design variables
- `YY1`: family identifier
- `Y1`: implicate identifier (1–5)
- `wt1b1`–`wt1b999`: bootstrap replicate weights
- `mm1`–`mm999`: multiplicity factors

## Implicates
Five implicates. All five are mandatory for publishable inference. Multiple-imputation variance = sample variance across implicate estimates.

## Replicate weights
999 bootstrap replicates. Sampling variance = sample variance across replicate estimates using `wt1bN * mmN`.

## Geography and years
National. The retained analysis uses 2022; the configured historical series
is triennial from 1989 through 2022.

## Known breaks and limitations
- High-wealth oversample from IRS SOI data (confidential; not in PUF).
- List sample coverage changes across waves.
- Top-coding applied to protect confidentiality.
- 2022 SCF fielded during a period of high inflation.

## Validated release used by the retained analysis
The retained SCF analysis records release `scf-2022-7b35624679b4` in its
diagnostics. It reproduces median family net worth at $192,084 against the
Federal Reserve's $192,900 benchmark, a 0.42% difference.
