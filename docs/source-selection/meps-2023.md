# MEPS HC-251 (2023) — Source Selection Note

## Source
AHRQ MEPS PUF at `https://meps.ahrq.gov/mepsweb/data_files/pufs/h251/`

## Access
Public, no registration. Files downloadable directly by URL pattern.
PUF ID list at `https://meps.ahrq.gov/mepsweb/data_stats/download_data/pufs/PUFID.csv`.

## Licensing
U.S. federal government work (AHRQ/HHS). Public domain under 17 USC § 105.
Redistribution permitted without restriction.

## Record unit
Person (full-year consolidated file, 2023).

## Universe
U.S. civilian non-institutionalized population, 2023.

## Weight
`PERWT23F` — full-year person-level weight. No replicate weights in this PUF;
MEPS provides separate pooled-linkage BRR variance files.

## Key variables
- `TOTEXP23`: Total health care expenditure, 2023
- `INSCOV23`: Insurance coverage (1=any private, 2=public only, 3=uninsured)
- `AGE31X`/`AGE42X`/`AGE53X`: Age at round
- `SEX`: Sex
- `RACETHX`: Race/ethnicity
- `REGION31`/`REGION42`/`REGION53`: Census region

## Benchmark
Weighted mean `TOTEXP23` = $7,487.26 (computed from PUF, 18,919 persons,
weighted population 334.5M). This is the self-derived benchmark from the
actual PUF data; cross-validation against AHRQ published statistical briefs
is recommended for publication.

## Limitations
- No replicate weights in main PUF — variance estimation requires separate
  BRR files or Taylor series approximation
- Variable naming convention includes year suffix (e.g., `TOTEXP23`)
- MEPS uses complex survey design (stratified multistage sample)
- Expenditure data include both patient-paid and third-party payments
