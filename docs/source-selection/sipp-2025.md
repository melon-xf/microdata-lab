# Source selection: 2025 SIPP person-month income and program participation

Decision date: 2026-08-02

## Concept and estimand

Select the Census Bureau's 2025 Survey of Income and Program Participation (SIPP) for analyses of monthly income, program participation, economic transitions, and within-person change during the January–December 2024 reference period. The public-use record unit is a person-month. Unique people are identified by `SSUID+PNUM`; primary and replicate files merge on `SSUID+PNUM+MONTHCODE`.

## Local catalog comparison at selection time

The local catalog had no match for “program participation” or “longitudinal.” It found annual or subjective adjacent measures, but they are not equivalent:

| Source | Record unit and concept | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SIPP 2025 | Person-month income, program participation, household and family dynamics; follows panel members across waves | Selected for monthly transitions and participation spells |
| CPS ASEC 2025 | Person records with annual income and poverty measures | Available but not comparable for month-to-month transitions |
| ACS PUMS 2024 | Person/household annual characteristics and income | Available but cross-sectional and not comparable for longitudinal dynamics |
| SCF 2022 | Primary economic unit balance sheet and annual income | Available but not comparable for program spells or person-month income |
| SHED 2025 | Adult respondent subjective financial well-being and income variability questions | Available but not a detailed monthly income/program record |
| ATUS 2024 | Person-day time diary | Available but not comparable for income or program participation |

The desired concept was **not found** in the pre-SIPP local catalog. Related annual, wealth, subjective, and time-use measures were **not comparable**, rather than substitutes.

## Universe, geography, and period

- Universe: civilian noninstitutionalized population of the United States, subject to the detailed monthly in-survey-universe indicator `RIN_UNIV` and variable-specific universes.
- Record unit: person-month; do not interpret rows as unique people.
- Release year: 2025 SIPP.
- Reference period: January through December 2024.
- Geography: use only public-use geography documented by Census; no unsupported small-area inference.
- Panels represented in the release: 2022–2025, waves 1–4 as applicable.

## Weight and variance design

- Primary monthly person weight: `WPFINWGT`; zero-weight person-months remain in the primary file but are not in the cross-sectional replicate-weight cohort.
- Replicate full-sample weight: `REPWGT0`.
- Variance replicates: `REPWGT1` through `REPWGT240`.
- Merge primary and replicate records on `SSUID+PNUM+MONTHCODE` exactly as the official Census reader example specifies.
- Variance formula: `1 / (240 * 0.5^2)` times the sum of squared replicate-estimate deviations from the point estimate.
- Longitudinal estimates require the separately published year-group longitudinal weights and longitudinal replicate weights. The initial cross-sectional adapter does not silently substitute `WPFINWGT` for those designs.

## Imputation and missingness

The data dictionary defines variable-level allocation/status flags. Analyses must distinguish imputed values, logical or structural states, not-in-universe codes, and ordinary missing values. Numeric code meaning must come from the 2025 dictionary or online codebook, never from a mnemonic alone.

## Validation benchmark

The release gate reproduces Census’s official input-validation statistics for `TPTOTINC`, including 323,731 nonmissing observations and an unweighted mean of 4,960.603764. It also requires:

- 379,215 primary person-month rows and 5,203 primary columns;
- 378,291 positive-weight replicate rows and 246 replicate-file columns;
- `REPWGT0` plus all 240 variance replicates;
- exact equality between the positive-`WPFINWGT` primary keys and replicate keys;
- row-level agreement between `WPFINWGT` and `REPWGT0`;
- all 12 reference months, documented panel/wave domains, and official validation-workbook statistics.

This benchmark validates source ingestion. Any substantive analysis must additionally reproduce a topic-specific official estimate before publication.

## Limitations

- Census reports that the 2025 SIPP continued to face increasing-cost complications and a generally lower-than-average national unit response rate.
- SIPP is longitudinal, but attrition and weight selection depend on the estimand and observed-year group.
- Public-use geography is limited.
- Monthly and annual values are not interchangeable; timing, reference period, and annualization must be explicit.

## Provenance and promotion contract

The adapter discovers only the official Census release page and
`www2.census.gov` artifacts. Required artifacts include pipe-delimited
primary data, cross-sectional replicate weights, both schemas, both
validation workbooks, the data dictionary, release notes, the official
Python reader example, and the 2025 Users' Guide. Promotion is blocked until
every artifact, schema, cohort, benchmark, normalization, and integrity gate
passes. Run `uv run microdata status` for host-local release state.
