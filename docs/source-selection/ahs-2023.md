# Source selection: 2023 AHS national housing conditions and costs

Decision date: 2026-08-02

## Concept and estimand

Select the 2023 American Housing Survey Integrated National Sample PUF revision 1.1 for descriptive estimates of housing-stock characteristics, physical condition, tenure, housing costs, mortgages, residents, and remodeling projects. The canonical benchmark estimand is occupied housing units (`INTSTATUS = '1'`).

## Alternatives checked in the local catalog

- **SCF 2022:** Contains household balance-sheet concepts such as home value, mortgages, and home equity. It does not provide the AHS housing-unit condition, structure, neighborhood, and project detail and is not an equivalent source for housing-stock estimates.
- **ACS PUMS 2024:** Supports annual person/household population and selected housing characteristics at broader geography. It is useful for demographic and geographic estimates but is not a substitute for AHS's detailed housing-condition instrument.
- **SHED 2025:** Measures perceived financial challenges, including housing costs. It does not measure the national housing stock or detailed unit condition.
- **Local catalog at selection time:** No validated release matched the exact housing-condition concept.

## Record unit and universe

The release is relational: one row per sampled housing unit in `household`, one row per person in `person`, one row per mortgage in `mortgage`, and one row per reported remodeling project in `project`. `CONTROL` joins child records to housing units. Mortgage and person rows have documented composite keys; project rows have no stable unique project identifier and are not assigned a synthetic semantic key.

The selected universe is the 2023 Integrated National Sample covering residential housing units in the 50 states and District of Columbia. The independently selected metropolitan sample is not combined with the national file.

## Geography and period

This first slice is the 2023 national release, revision 1.1. AHS is generally biennial. Geography below the published PUF identifiers is not inferred.

## Weights, design, and imputation

- Use `WEIGHT` for estimates based on full-sample variables.
- Use `SP1WEIGHT` or `SP2WEIGHT` for the corresponding split-sample module variables; zero split weights are structural and not ordinary missing values.
- Use all 160 Fay BRR weights `REPWEIGHT1` through `REPWEIGHT160`.
- Apply the official variance factor `4 / 160 = 0.025`; report 90% uncertainty with the documented AHS convention where required.
- Preserve J-prefixed edit/imputation flags and distinguish not reported from not applicable.

## Benchmark and provenance

The official 2023 Table Specifications and PUF Estimates workbook reports 133,231 thousand occupied housing units with a 90% margin of error of 381 thousand. The relational PUF reproduces 133,230.777 thousand and a replicate-weight MOE of 381.054 thousand, rounding exactly to those values.

The release retains the CSV archive, revision record, verification workbook, value-label package, national design/weighting report, replicate-weight guide, and the official machine-readable codebook JSON.

## Breaks and limitations

The 2023 national unit response rate was 59.2%, below the historical average. Estimates are descriptive and subject to sampling and nonsampling error. National and metropolitan releases have different sample structures and must not be silently pooled. Comparisons across redesign periods require the year-specific technical documentation and variable-history records.
